# Giggletwig — Monument of Triumph Community DIM Wishlists

This is a separate, curated wishlist collection for weapons refreshed or reprised as part of Destiny 2 Update 9.7.0: Monument of Triumph.

## Files
- `monument-pve.txt` — PvE recommendations.
- `monument-pvp.txt` — PvP recommendations.
- `monument-audit.xlsx` — recommendation evidence, exact manifest hashes, confidence and source links.

## Scope and selection
Monument of Triumph was a broad loot refresh, not a single seasonal weapon set. This starter collection covers a curated selection across:
- Pantheon / reprised raid weapons
- Grasp of Avarice dungeon weapons
- Crucible focusing
- Trials of Osiris
- Cosmodrome destination refresh

It does not claim to cover every weapon changed by Monument of Triumph. Weapons are included where a specific community-supported roll could be identified. More weapons can be added as stronger evidence is found.

## DIM matching approach
Each entry specifies the two recommended trait perks (columns 3 and 4) only. Barrel and magazine are intentionally unrestricted. DIM supports one or more perk hashes per line, so this lets the wishlist recognize the recommended trait pairing without inventing a barrel/magazine combination. DIM automatically matches enhanced versions when the regular perk hash is used.

The exact weapon item hashes and trait plug hashes were checked against the supplied Bungie manifest export dated 2026-11-20. The files passed local syntax and item-type/hash validation. They have not been imported into a live DIM session in this workflow.

## Confidence
- **High**: current Monument-era guide plus corroborating community usage or another independent recommendation.
- **Medium**: one credible guide or community wishlist recommendation; alternatives may represent a different playstyle.
- **Niche**: useful for a specialist role, not a universal god roll.
